# Calendar — ten uses that actually happen

A calendar is not "dates as boxes" — it is **the things of a span of time laid
out**: who is on which day, at what hour, in which cell. The ten cases below
use real or attributable data — the 2026 holidays come from the State Council's
notice of 2025-11-04, and the timetable and schedule follow how school and
work actually run.

Every one is a URL: paste it into an issue or a README, change a number any
time.

## What these ten say

- **One grid, seven shapes.** Month/week/year/two months/three months are
  "date × date"; the timetable and the schedule are "period × day" — all grids,
  only the axes differ.
- **The content is in the URL, not in the code.** Swapping an item means
  swapping a link.
- **Holidays are data, not logic.** The make-up workdays
  (`day=…;workday`) and the holidays (`day=…;holiday`) both live in the link —
  when the 2026 arrangement changes, the link changes with it.
